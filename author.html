Aric Wilmunder

Author, historical researcher, and former Lucasfilm Games software engineer whose work combines original archival research, museum collaboration, interactive media, and historical storytelling.

Aric Wilmunder and his wife, Lori Love, stand before the historic Pownalborough Court House in Dresden, Maine. Behind them are Beckie, Louisa, and Sallie Prescott, imagined as they may have appeared during the 1830s. The courthouse, the Prescott sisters, and the family’s surviving schoolgirl samplers inspired The Prescott Girls .

Biography

Aric Wilmunder is the author of The Prescott Girls: A Letter from Philadelphia , a middle-grade historical novel inspired by real nineteenth-century schoolgirl samplers and the lives of three sisters connected to the historic Pownalborough Court House in Dresden, Maine.

Before turning to historical writing, he spent decades in technology and interactive entertainment, including work at Lucasfilm Games, later LucasArts. His background in software engineering and interactive media continues to shape his research-driven approach to storytelling, historical interpretation, and visual reconstruction.

Wilmunder works with historians, museums, and textile experts. With Lori Love, an accomplished needleworker whose work has received multiple honors, he helped document and return historically significant samplers connected to the Betsy Ross family. Their research appeared in PieceWork magazine in the article Six Stitches Through Time .

Current Projects

  • The Prescott Girls : Middle-grade historical fiction inspired by nineteenth-century schoolgirl samplers.
  • Museum Coloring Book & Activity Guide (in development) : A companion activity book for young visitors to the Pownalborough Court House Museum.
  • Historic Reimaginings : Research into responsible AI-assisted historical interpretation grounded in documented people, places, and artifacts.
  • Teacher Resources : Free classroom materials for grades 4–7 connecting literature with history, geography, artifact analysis, and critical thinking.

Historical Research

Original research into nineteenth-century schoolgirl samplers led to the identification and return of historically significant Prescott family artifacts to Maine, collaborations with historians and museums, publication in PieceWork magazine, and ultimately the creation of The Prescott Girls.
